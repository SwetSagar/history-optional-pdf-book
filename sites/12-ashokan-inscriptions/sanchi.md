- **Location & Setting**: Situated in Raisen district.
- **Periodization & Excavation**: Multi-layered occupation phase belonging to Ashokan Inscriptions.
- **Material Culture & Finds**: Yields architectural and epigraphic remains (The stupa was originally commissioned by the emperor in the third century BC, expanded by the Shungas with stone slabs, and its gateways were improved in the Satavahana period.; The pillar is of finely polished sandstone and carries an inscription of Ashoka and another of the Gupta period.).
- **Historical Significance**: Key reference site for studying regional socio-economic development and historical sequence in Ashokan Inscriptions.
