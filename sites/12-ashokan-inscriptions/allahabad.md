The site of a non-Ashokan inscription of the fourth century CE, of the Gupta emperor Samudragupta, composed by Harishena.

- It is written in Sanskrit in a refined Brahmi script and lists Samudragupta's political and military achievements.
- It is now known as Prayagraj.
- It is known in the older sources as Prayaga.
