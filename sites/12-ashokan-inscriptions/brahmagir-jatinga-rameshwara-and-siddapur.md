- **Location & Setting**: Situated in Karnataka.
- **Periodization & Excavation**: Multi-layered occupation phase belonging to Ashokan Inscriptions.
- **Material Culture & Finds**: Yields ceramic assemblage, lithic/stone tool industry (The Neolithic settlement had wattle-and-daub huts on wooden or bamboo posts, ground and polished stone tools, microlithic blades and grey pottery; adults were buried extended and children in urns.; The megalithic phase yielded iron implements and weapons, black-and-red ware with graffiti, shell bangles, and beads of terracotta, shell and jasper, together with matt-painted buff ware similar to Jorwe ware, and stone cist burials.).
- **Historical Significance**: Key reference site for studying regional socio-economic development and historical sequence in Ashokan Inscriptions.
