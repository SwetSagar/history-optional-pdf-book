- **Location & Setting**: Situated in Maharashtra, Hazara district.
- **Periodization & Excavation**: Multi-layered occupation phase belonging to Ashokan Inscriptions.
- **Material Culture & Finds**: Yields ceramic assemblage, architectural and epigraphic remains (It is associated with the Satavahanas and then the Guptas, and is mentioned in the Periplus.; Arab sources describe it as an important centre of artisanal activity.).
- **Historical Significance**: Key reference site for studying regional socio-economic development and historical sequence in Ashokan Inscriptions.
