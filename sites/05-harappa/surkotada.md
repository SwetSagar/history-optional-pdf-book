A Mature to Late Harappan site in Gujarat with no break or desertion in its occupation.

- A citadel is present from the earliest level.
- In the later phase a new pottery culture appears, though the structure of the settlement is unchanged; the site may have served as a regional capital or garrison town.
- Faunal remains include mongoose, kept to guard against snakes, along with elephant and wolf bones, and remains of the horse.
