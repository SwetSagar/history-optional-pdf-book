- **Location & Setting**: Situated in Haryana, Fatehabad district, Hakra River.
- **Periodization & Excavation**: Multi-layered occupation phase belonging to Harappa.
- **Material Culture & Finds**: Yields ceramic assemblage (Its mud bricks are in the ratio 1:2:3, and a house complex of six rooms with a courtyard and chulha was excavated.; Copper arrowheads, rings and bangles were recovered, along with beads of carnelian, jasper and agate, and a pit used either for sacrifices or for industrial activity.).
- **Historical Significance**: Key reference site for studying regional socio-economic development and historical sequence in Harappa.
