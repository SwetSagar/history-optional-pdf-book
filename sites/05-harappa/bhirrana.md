An Early and Mature Harappan site in Haryana, also a Neolithic–Chalcolithic site yielding Hakra ware.

- Its mud bricks are in the ratio 1:2:3, and a house complex of six rooms with a courtyard and chulha was excavated.
- Copper arrowheads, rings and bangles were recovered, along with beads of carnelian, jasper and agate, and a pit used either for sacrifices or for industrial activity.
