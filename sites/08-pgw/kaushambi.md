The capital of Vatsa, a branch of the Kurus, in Uttar Pradesh.

- The settlement was well planned and fortified, its early houses of timber and later of brick.
- Agricultural equipment and iron weapons have been found.
- The Ghoshitarama monastery was located here, and monastic seals of a later period bear the name of the monastery.
- It is known in the older sources as Kosambi.
