A Mesolithic site in Uttar Pradesh with distinct habitation and butchering areas.

- Its microliths are of chert, quartz, chalcedony, crystal and agate.
- Twenty-eight elliptical burials were found, including a child burial and two in which a man and a woman were buried together; grave goods comprise microliths, shells, bone arrowheads, rings and ochre.
- The people were tall and had good dental health, but suffered from osteoarthritis.
