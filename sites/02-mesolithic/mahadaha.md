- **Location & Setting**: Situated in Uttar Pradesh, Pratapgarh district.
- **Periodization & Excavation**: Multi-layered occupation phase belonging to Mesolithic.
- **Material Culture & Finds**: Yields lithic/stone tool industry (Its microliths are of chert, quartz, chalcedony, crystal and agate.; Twenty-eight elliptical burials were found, including a child burial and two in which a man and a woman were buried together; grave goods comprise microliths, shells, bone arrowheads, rings and ochre.).
- **Historical Significance**: Key reference site for studying regional socio-economic development and historical sequence in Mesolithic.
