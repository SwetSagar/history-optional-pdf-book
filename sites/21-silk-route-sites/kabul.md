A city regarded, with Qandahar, as one of the two gateways to India, and central to Mughal policy in the north-west.

- The Mughals maintained a strong administration here and sought where possible to keep control of Qandahar, using diplomacy to prevent a hostile combination of powers arising in West Asia.
- It was held under a governor; Munim Khan, a close confidant of Humayun, governed here.
