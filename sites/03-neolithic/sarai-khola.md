- **Location & Setting**: Situated in Pakistan.
- **Periodization & Excavation**: Multi-layered occupation phase belonging to Neolithic.
- **Material Culture & Finds**: Yields ceramic assemblage, lithic/stone tool industry (Period I produced handmade plain red or brown burnished pottery, ground and polished stone celts, blades, microliths and numerous bone points, together with terracotta wheels and toy carts.; Period II is Early Harappan and shows the transition from pit dwellings to mud-brick houses, with Kot Dijian pottery, microliths, celts and chisels.).
- **Historical Significance**: Key reference site for studying regional socio-economic development and historical sequence in Neolithic.
