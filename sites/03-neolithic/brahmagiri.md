A site in Karnataka with Neolithic, megalithic and Ashokan remains.

- The Neolithic settlement had wattle-and-daub huts on wooden or bamboo posts, ground and polished stone tools, microlithic blades and grey pottery; adults were buried extended and children in urns.
- The megalithic phase yielded iron implements and weapons, black-and-red ware with graffiti, shell bangles, and beads of terracotta, shell and jasper, together with matt-painted buff ware similar to Jorwe ware, and stone cist burials.
- Ashokan Minor Rock Edicts I and II were found here, addressed to the officer of Suvarnagiri; the locality marked the southernmost extent of the Mauryan empire.
