- **Location & Setting**: Situated in Pakistan, Bolan river.
- **Periodization & Excavation**: Multi-layered occupation phase belonging to Neolithic.
- **Material Culture & Finds**: Yields ceramic assemblage (In Periods I and II, the Neolithic, houses were built of handmade mud bricks with rectangular rooms, and granaries have been identified; the transition from hunting to animal domestication is recorded here.; Burials were in oval pits with the body bent, with grave goods such as lapis lazuli, goats and beads, and secondary burials had the bones covered in red ochre.).
- **Historical Significance**: Key reference site for studying regional socio-economic development and historical sequence in Neolithic.
