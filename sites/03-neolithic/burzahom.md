- **Location & Setting**: Situated in Jammu and Kashmir, Kashmir valley.
- **Periodization & Excavation**: Multi-layered occupation phase belonging to Neolithic.
- **Material Culture & Finds**: Yields ceramic assemblage, lithic/stone tool industry (Its inhabitants lived in mud-plastered pit dwellings, round or oval and wider towards the base, with square or rectangular pits towards the centre; charcoal, ash, potsherds and stone hearths were recovered.; A well-developed bone tool industry produced harpoons, needles and awls.).
- **Historical Significance**: Key reference site for studying regional socio-economic development and historical sequence in Neolithic.
