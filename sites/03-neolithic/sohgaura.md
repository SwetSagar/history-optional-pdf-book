- **Location & Setting**: Situated in Uttar Pradesh.
- **Periodization & Excavation**: Multi-layered occupation phase belonging to Neolithic.
- **Material Culture & Finds**: Yields architectural and epigraphic remains (It records an order issued from Manavasiti by the mahamatras of Shravasti that, during an outbreak of drought, grain be distributed and not withheld.; The order may be that of Chandragupta.).
- **Historical Significance**: Key reference site for studying regional socio-economic development and historical sequence in Neolithic.
