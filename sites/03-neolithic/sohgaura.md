A site in Uttar Pradesh bearing a non-Ashokan inscription on bronze, in four lines, in Prakrit and the Brahmi script.

- It records an order issued from Manavasiti by the mahamatras of Shravasti that, during an outbreak of drought, grain be distributed and not withheld.
- The order may be that of Chandragupta.
