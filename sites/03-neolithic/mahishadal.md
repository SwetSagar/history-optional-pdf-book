A largely Chalcolithic site in West Bengal near the Kopai river, its period running from 1600 to 1415 BC.

- Finds include microliths, bone tools, beads of steatite and semi-precious stone, terracotta bangles and a phallus, one copper celt, and black-and-red ware.
- Terracotta figurines and clay seals bearing symbolic signs were also recovered.
