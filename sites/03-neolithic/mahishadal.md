- **Location & Setting**: Situated in West Bengal, Kopai river.
- **Periodization & Excavation**: Multi-layered occupation phase belonging to Neolithic.
- **Material Culture & Finds**: Yields ceramic assemblage, lithic/stone tool industry, architectural and epigraphic remains (Finds include microliths, bone tools, beads of steatite and semi-precious stone, terracotta bangles and a phallus, one copper celt, and black-and-red ware.; Terracotta figurines and clay seals bearing symbolic signs were also recovered.).
- **Historical Significance**: Key reference site for studying regional socio-economic development and historical sequence in Neolithic.
