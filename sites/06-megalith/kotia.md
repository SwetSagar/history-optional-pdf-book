- **Location**: Kotia, in the Belan valley of south-eastern Uttar Pradesh (the Prayagraj-Mirzapur tract), on the northern Vindhyan fringe.
- **Period/Level**: Megalithic, dated c. 800-300 BCE - later than the pre-iron megaliths around it, and the single iron-bearing exception in the province. Investigated in the Allahabad University programme of Belan valley excavations.
- **Key Finds & Significance**: The exception that carries iron in a largely pre-iron megalithic region, and therefore the site that dates the arrival of iron in the Vindhyas.
  - Many iron tools - a spearhead, two sickles, an arrowhead and an adze - which Upinder Singh reads as indicating advanced metallurgical technique.
  - The graves yielded few human skeletal remains, but three contained the remains of domesticated sheep, pig and cattle; cut marks show the animals were killed at the time of burial.
  - Thick-fabric pottery: black-and-red ware, red ware, black-slipped ware and a dull coarse black or grey ware. Bones of ox, sheep and pig, some with cut marks, occur in the habitation deposit.
  - *Sources*: Upinder Singh, ch. 5.
