An important trade centre under the Shungas, Nagas, Satavahanas and Guptas, in Madhya Pradesh;

- Ashoka served as its governor.
- The Heliodorus Pillar is located here, constructed around 110 BC by the Greek ambassador at the court of the Shunga king Bhagabhadra and dedicated to Vasudeva; its script is Brahmi and its language Prakrit.
