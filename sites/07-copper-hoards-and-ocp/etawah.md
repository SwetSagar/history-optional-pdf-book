- **Location**: Saipai, Etawah district, Uttar Pradesh, in the lower Ganga-Yamuna doab near the Yamuna, in the eastern OCP zone.
- **Period/Level**: An excavated OCP settlement of the 2nd millennium BCE, within the thermoluminescence range of 2650-1180 BCE obtained for the OCP culture. Excavated by the UP State Archaeology Department (1970s).
- **Key Finds & Significance**: The single most important copper hoard site, because here alone the objects came out of a dated, excavated OCP level rather than a chance find.
  - A copper hooked spearhead and a harpoon were recovered from the OCP deposit itself - the evidence that ties the copper hoards to the OCP culture and dates them. Anthropomorphs, harpoons, hooked swords, lance heads and thick, glossy, light-green axes are reported from the district.
  - Sandstone pounders, querns and pestles, and bones of domesticated Bos indicus, show a settled farming and cattle-keeping village behind the metal.
  - Saipai belongs to the eastern OCP zone, which - unlike Bargaon, Ambakheri and Jodhpura in the west - shows no link with the Harappan ceramic tradition. Upinder Singh singles out the site as 'especially important' for precisely this stratified association.
  - *Sources*: Upinder Singh, ch. 5; ASI, *IAR*.
