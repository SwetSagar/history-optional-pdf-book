An ochre-coloured pottery site in Uttar Pradesh excavated by B. B. Lal.

- Its pottery is ill-fired, thick, ochre-washed and rolled, and shows similarity with the lowest level of the Hastinapur ceramic industry.
- Nine flat celts and harpoons were also recovered.
