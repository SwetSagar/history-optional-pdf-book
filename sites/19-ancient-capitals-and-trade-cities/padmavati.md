The seat of a line of Naga kings, identified with modern Pawaya in Gwalior district, Madhya Pradesh.

- The Puranas record the rule of a line of nine Naga kings from here.
- Several Naga kings are known from inscriptions, coins, seals and sealings, and coins bearing the name of a ruler titled maharaja have been found.
