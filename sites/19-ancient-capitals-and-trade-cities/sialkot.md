The capital of the Indo-Greek king Menander, about 200 BCE, and an important centre of Buddhism. It was the easternmost outpost of Hellenic expansion before 327 BC and an important trade centre; large quantities of Greek coins, ancient Zoroastrian temples and many Buddhist stupas have been found here.
