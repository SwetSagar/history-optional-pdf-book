An international port since pre-Mauryan days, in Gujarat, serving the Sakas, Kushanas and Satavahanas.

- Bead making and ring wells were important here.
- Two silk routes from the north served it by way of the Uttarapatha: one from Taxila through Punjab and the western part of the Yamuna, following the Yamuna to Mathura and Ujjain to Broach, and another from Taxila through the lower Indus basin to Broach.
- It is known in the older sources as Barygaza.
