An important Chera port of the Sangam age, on the banks of the Periyar river at Cranganore, Kerala, and capital of the Perumal Chera kings.

- It was a major centre exporting black pepper, along with spices and precious and semi-precious stones.
- The Romans built two outposts and a temple of Augustus here to protect their interests.
- It is mentioned in Greek literature and by Pliny and the Periplus.
- Documents recording a contract between an Alexandrian importer of pepper and a local financier have been recovered, indicating a large volume of trade.
