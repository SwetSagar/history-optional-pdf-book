- **Location**: Situated in the Thar Desert region around Ajmer and the Budha Pushkar / Hokra lake basins in Rajasthan.
- **Period/Level**: Multi-layered sequence covering the Lower, Middle, and Upper Palaeolithic phases.
- **Key Finds & Significance**: The Budha Pushkar and Hokra basins provide a continuous sequence of prehistoric human occupation from the Lower Palaeolithic onwards.
  - Excavations revealed Middle Palaeolithic working floors at Hokra and Baridhani, situated close to ancient, now dried-up freshwater lake beds.
  - Small factory and camp sites in this region indicate a cooler, wetter, and more humid palaeo-environment with abundant surface water during the Middle Palaeolithic phase.
  - *Sources*: Upinder Singh, ch. 2.
