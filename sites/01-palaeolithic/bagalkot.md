- **Location**: Bagalkot district, northern Karnataka, in the Malaprabha-Ghataprabha basin of the Krishna system.
- **Period/Level**: Lower to Middle Palaeolithic, part of the stratified Lower-to-Upper Palaeolithic sequence identified in the Malaprabha-Ghataprabha valleys.
- **Key Finds & Significance**: An early-to-middle Palaeolithic tool zone dominated by Acheulian handaxes and cleavers, with pebble tools such as choppers and chopping tools occurring alongside them.
  - Kavalli nearby is an open-air factory site, where tools survive in successive stages of preparation close to the raw material source.
  - The co-occurrence of pebble tools with handaxes here helped overturn the older view that the southern Lower Palaeolithic, then called "Madrasian", was distinct from the pebble-tool traditions of the north-west.
  - The same district later became the heartland of Chalukyan temple architecture at Badami, Aihole and Pattadakal on the Malaprabha.
