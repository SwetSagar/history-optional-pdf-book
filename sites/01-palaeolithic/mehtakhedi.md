- **Location**: Mehtakheri, Khargone district, Madhya Pradesh, in the middle Narmada valley.
- **Period/Level**: Upper Palaeolithic; a piece of ostrich eggshell from the site has been dated to over 41,900 BP, with artefacts in the range of about 50,000 years ago.
- **Key Finds & Significance**: Mehtakheri has produced one of the earliest dated microblade industries in India, and is therefore central to the debate on when microlithic technology, and with it anatomically modern humans, appeared in the subcontinent.
  - The ostrich eggshell fragment is part of the wider Indian evidence that Struthio roamed many parts of Asia, including India, until the end of the Pleistocene or the early Holocene.
  - About 50 such discoveries have been made across Rajasthan, Madhya Pradesh and Maharashtra, with dates from Chandresal in Rajasthan of 38,900 and 36,500 BP and from Ramnagar in Madhya Pradesh of over 31,000 BP.
