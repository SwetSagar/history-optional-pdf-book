- **Location & Setting**: Situated in Madhya Pradesh, middle valley.
- **Periodization & Excavation**: Multi-layered occupation phase belonging to Palaeolithic.
- **Material Culture & Finds**: Yields lithic/stone tool industry, architectural and epigraphic remains (Cranial fragments, collar bones and animal bones of the Middle to Late Palaeolithic were recovered.; Tools were made of limestone, sandstone, quartzite, dolerite and chert, and huge granite blocks were used to support temporary shelters.).
- **Historical Significance**: Key reference site for studying regional socio-economic development and historical sequence in Palaeolithic.
