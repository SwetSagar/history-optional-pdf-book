- **Location**: The Hiran river valley in Saurashtra, Gujarat, draining the southern flank of the Gir hills.
- **Period/Level**: Lower Palaeolithic dated between 190,000 and 69,000 BP, and Middle Palaeolithic dated 56,800 BP, both by the uranium-thorium series method.
- **Key Finds & Significance**: One of the very few sequences in Gujarat to carry absolute dates for both the Lower and the Middle Palaeolithic, giving Saurashtra a securely fixed Pleistocene framework.
  - The dates place the Hiran valley alongside Didwana in Rajasthan, the Son valley in Madhya Pradesh and Nevasa in Maharashtra as the small group of Indian localities where Lower Palaeolithic contexts have been dated absolutely rather than by typology alone.
  - Lower Palaeolithic tools elsewhere in Gujarat come from the Sabarmati and its Orsang and Karjan tributaries and from the Bhadar valley, so the Hiran dates supply the chronology for that wider regional record.
