A site in the Tapti valley, Maharashtra, showing a stratigraphic sequence of Middle Palaeolithic, Upper Palaeolithic and Mesolithic tools.

- A gradual reduction in tool size can be traced from the late Pleistocene, with microliths of that age.
- An ostrich eggshell engraved with a criss-cross pattern was recovered, together with beads made from the shell.
