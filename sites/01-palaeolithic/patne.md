- **Location & Setting**: Situated in Maharashtra, Tapti valley.
- **Periodization & Excavation**: Multi-layered occupation phase belonging to Palaeolithic.
- **Material Culture & Finds**: Yields lithic/stone tool industry (A gradual reduction in tool size can be traced from the late Pleistocene, with microliths of that age.; An ostrich eggshell engraved with a criss-cross pattern was recovered, together with beads made from the shell.).
- **Historical Significance**: Key reference site for studying regional socio-economic development and historical sequence in Palaeolithic.
