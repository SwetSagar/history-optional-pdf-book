- **Location**: Gudiyam cave, in the hills of the Kortallayar basin, Tiruvallur district, not far from Chennai, Tamil Nadu.
- **Period/Level**: A sequence of Lower, Middle and Upper Palaeolithic tools.
- **Key Finds & Significance**: The cave has yielded a complete Lower-to-Upper Palaeolithic tool sequence, one of the few cave stratigraphies available in South India.
  - The small number of tools and the absence of other remains suggest that the site was occupied only for short periods at a time, a transit or seasonal shelter rather than a base camp.
  - With Attirampakkam and Pallavaram nearby, Gudiyam makes the Kortallayar basin the classic stratified Palaeolithic region of Tamil Nadu, giving a sequence from the early Palaeolithic to the Mesolithic near Chennai.
