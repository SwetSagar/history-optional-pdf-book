- **Location**: Mogara hill near Jodhpur, western Rajasthan, on the eastern fringe of the Thar desert.
- **Period/Level**: Lower, Middle and Upper Palaeolithic, and Mesolithic.
- **Key Finds & Significance**: The hill appears to have been a factory site where tools of all four phases were made, showing that the same raw material source was returned to across several hundred thousand years.
  - Factory sites of this kind are generally located close to sources of raw material and are marked by a profusion of stone tools in various stages of preparation.
  - Such long-lived quarry-workshops, like those in the Sukkur and Rohri hills of upper Sindh, indicate that knowledge of good stone sources was transmitted across an enormous span of prehistoric time.
