- **Location**: Odai, Villupuram district, Tamil Nadu, near Tiruvakkarai in the South Pennar tract.
- **Period/Level**: Middle to Upper Palaeolithic with microliths; the hominid remains belong to the middle to upper Pleistocene.
- **Key Finds & Significance**: One of the very few hominid fossil localities in the subcontinent, marked alongside Hathnora on standard maps of hominid discoveries in South Asia.
  - A skull cap of the middle to upper Pleistocene was recovered here, and a complete fossilised human infant skull was found in association with microliths, dated to about 166,000 years ago.
  - Petrified wood fossils roughly 20 million years old also occur; the trees are thought to have been transported to the spot before being petrified.
  - With Tiruvakkarai nearby, Odai makes this stretch of northern Tamil Nadu one of the richest prehistoric and palaeontological landscapes in the state.
