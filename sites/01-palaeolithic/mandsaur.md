- **Location & Setting**: Situated in Madhya Pradesh, Narmada River.
- **Periodization & Excavation**: Multi-layered occupation phase belonging to Palaeolithic.
- **Material Culture & Finds**: Yields architectural and epigraphic remains (At Daraki Chattan more than five hundred pieces of rock art have been found together with hammer stones; dated to between 200,000 and 500,000 years ago, it is among the oldest such sites in India.; The site also carries non-Ashokan inscriptions: one in Sanskrit, dated to the sixth century, was issued by Yasodharman to mark his victory over the Huna king Mihirakula; another, of Kumaragupta in the fifth century, records the migration of silk weavers from Lata to Dasapura, who organised themselves into a guild and built a sun temple.).
- **Historical Significance**: Key reference site for studying regional socio-economic development and historical sequence in Palaeolithic.
