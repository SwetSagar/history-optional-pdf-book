- **Location & Setting**: Situated in Bihar.
- **Periodization & Excavation**: Multi-layered occupation phase belonging to Palaeolithic.
- **Material Culture & Finds**: Yields lithic/stone tool industry (Broken pieces of stone and anvils were recovered, along with eight post holes marking the placing of wooden poles to support thatched huts.; Large and small fireplaces occur close together, and the site also served for stone working.).
- **Historical Significance**: Key reference site for studying regional socio-economic development and historical sequence in Palaeolithic.
