A site in Bihar with Lower Palaeolithic and Mesolithic occupation, preserving a living and working floor with both finished and unfinished artefacts.

- Broken pieces of stone and anvils were recovered, along with eight post holes marking the placing of wooden poles to support thatched huts.
- Large and small fireplaces occur close together, and the site also served for stone working.
