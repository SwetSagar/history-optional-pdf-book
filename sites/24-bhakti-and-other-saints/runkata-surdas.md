Associated with Surdas, a blind saint inspired by Vallabhacharya, who wrote in the Braj dialect.

- He composed the Sur Sagar, describing the love between Krishna and Radha, together with the Sur Saravali and the Sahitya Lahari.
- Some of his works appear in the Guru Granth Sahib.
